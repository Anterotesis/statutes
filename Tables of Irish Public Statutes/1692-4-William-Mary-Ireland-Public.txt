Table of Statutes passed in Ireland, 1692 4 William & Mary, c.1 - c.4.
From The Statutes at Large Passed in Ireland, vol. 3.
---

1. An Act of Recognition, of their Majesties undoubted Right to the Crown of Ireland.

2. An Act for Encouragement of Protestant Strangers to settle in this Kingdom of Ireland.

3. An Act for an additional Duty of Excise upon Beer, Ale, and other Liquors.

4. An Act for taking Affidavits in the Country, to be made Use of in the Courts of King’s Bench, Common Pleas, and Exchequer.
